The flood control scandal

Billions in flood-control funds were linked to ghost and substandard projects. Citizens, media and officials pushed for investigations and court cases. It tests targets 16.5, 16.6, 16.7 and 16.10.

  • Jul 28, 2025SONA: the President orders a probe of flood-control projects.
  • Aug 11, 2025The “Sumbong sa Pangulo” site lists 9,855 projects so citizens can report.
  • Sep 2025The Independent Commission for Infrastructure (ICI) is created.
  • Sep 21, 2025Mass anti-corruption rally at the People Power Monument.
  • Nov 2025The Ombudsman files the first cases at the Sandiganbayan.
  • Mar 31, 2026The ICI ends its work; court cases continue.

Accused persons are presumed innocent until proven guilty. Sources: Rappler, GMA News, Inquirer, PCO.

Bangsamoro votes for peace

After decades of conflict, the 2014 peace deal led to BARMM. On Sept 14, 2026, voters elected the region’s first parliament.

Voter turnout: 2.02 million of 2.39 million voters

MILF fighters decommissioned: about 26,000 of 40,000

  • 80 seats in the new parliament; lawmakers take office on Oct 30, 2026.
  • Police reported 16 election-related incidents, including 5 deaths.
  • About 14,000 fighters still await the final decommissioning phase.

Sources: GMA News, Philstar, Daily Tribune (Sept 2026).

Student council posts a monthly income-and-expense report.

Check two credible sources before posting news.

Know your rights

Learn how to file a request at foi.gov.ph and how barangay mediation works.

Stay safe online

Never send private photos; block and report; tell a trusted adult.

Our project: Tapat Tayo

An honest and safe campus campaign. Tapat means honest.

Objective

Build a campus culture of honesty, fairness and safety.

Who it is for

Students, student council, teachers and the guidance office.

Main activities

A transparency board with monthly funds reports, upstander and online-safety talks, a barangay assembly visit and an honesty pledge wall.

Expected results

Monthly funds reports, 100+ pledges, more bullying reports referred, and students who know how to use FOI and barangay mediation.

How we would run it in six weeks

  1. Week 1Plan and get adviser approval
  2. Week 2Orientation and pledge
  3. Weeks 3–4Run the activities
  4. Week 5Showcase and poster contest
  5. Week 6Evaluate and report
