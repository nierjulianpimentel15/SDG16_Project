What is SDG 16?

Promote peaceful and inclusive societies, give everyone access to justice, and build effective, accountable and inclusive institutions at all levels.

Three ideas in one goal

Peace

People are safe from violence, war and abuse at home, at school and online.

Justice

Everyone can reach fair courts, a lawyer and a legal identity, whatever their income.

Strong institutions

Government is honest, open and answerable, so people can trust it.
