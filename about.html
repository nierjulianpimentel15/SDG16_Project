About us

We are a group of students presenting Sustainable Development Goal 16 for our class, in a deck and on this website.

The group

Seven members. Each of us adds one short line on how Goal 16 touches our school, family or community.

About this project

Our class asked each group to pick one UN goal, research it and present it for the Philippines. We chose Goal 16 because trust in courts, officials and public safety shows up in daily life, from school funds to online safety.

The site follows the same order as our presentation: the goal, why it matters here, the causes, the laws, real stories, our own voices, actions students can take, our project idea and a call to action.
